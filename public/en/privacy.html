YOUR FILES. YOUR DEVICE.

Privacy, in plain English.

Your PDF stays in your browser. An optional account is a separate service.

Last updated October 19, 2026

Your PDF files

PDF selection, processing, preview, and download happen on your device. The compressor does not upload PDF contents, filenames, or page previews to our servers. File bytes are held in browser memory while processing. Downloaded files are saved by your browser. Closing the page clears its working memory; keep your original PDF until you have checked the result.

Optional accounts

You do not need an account to compress a PDF. If you register or sign in, account information travels over the network to the separately hosted authentication service and its database. This is different from PDF processing. Account information can include your email address, display name, password hash for email registration, sign-in provider identifiers, and session records. Your plaintext password is not stored in the database.

Google sign-in

If Google sign-in is configured, choosing it connects you to Google and the authentication service. Google supplies the basic identity information allowed by the sign-in flow, such as your name, email address, and profile image. Your Google password is entered with Google, not into this website. Google handles its own connection data under its privacy policy.

Browser storage and sessions

Authentication uses session cookies to keep you signed in. These are necessary for the optional account feature. This version does not include advertising, analytics trackers, or a server-side history of your PDF files.

Hosting information

The website host and authentication server receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. They may retain operational or security logs according to their configuration. This does not require receiving your PDF.

Control over your account

You can sign out to end your current session. Account registration is optional, and you can use the local compressor without it. Account information remains in the authentication database until removed by the service operator. Operational logs and database backups follow the server operator's retention settings.

Before you leave

Check every compressed file before using it, especially documents with signatures, forms, fine text, or important legal or medical information. Your original remains on your device.
