About compresser pdf

A PDF tool built to keep your documents on your device.

Updated October 20, 2026

What does compresser pdf offer?

compresser pdf is a browser-based PDF compression tool available in French and English. You can compress a document and compare sizes without an account; free sign-in is required to download the result.

How are our explanations prepared?

Our descriptions distinguish general PDF capabilities from functions actually implemented in this tool. The guide cites relevant technical documentation. Numerical limits describe software settings, not promised size reductions. Pages are updated when their content changes.

Where are documents processed?

Compression runs on your device. Accounts and account emails use a separate service and do not carry your PDF. Read our privacy policy and terms of use.
