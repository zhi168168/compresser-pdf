Which PDF compression mode should you choose?

Start with High quality for everyday documents. Choose Lossless to avoid deliberate image changes, or Smaller file when reducing size matters more than image detail.

Is your PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Compression happens locally in your browser, using your device’s processing power. The PDF is not uploaded, stored on our servers, or sent to an AI service.

You can compress and compare without signing in. Downloading requires sign-in. If you create an account, account details are sent to our authentication service. Your PDF remains local.

Does compression change PDF quality?

Image quality can change in High quality and Smaller file modes. Lossless avoids deliberate image changes. Native text and vectors stay in their original format; pages are not flattened into pictures.

Frequently asked questions

How can I compress a PDF for free?

Choose your file, run compression and compare the sizes. You can process the document without an account; sign in for free to download the result. Keep the original until you have checked the output.

Can I compress a PDF without registering?

Yes, for compression and comparison. Downloading requires sign-in. If you need a completely account-free workflow through to download, this service does not meet that requirement.

How can I compress a PDF to 100 KB, 300 KB or 500 KB?

No exact size is guaranteed. Try High quality, then Smaller file if needed, and compare the output bytes with the form’s limit. An illustrated report may not reach 100 KB while remaining readable.

Can I compress a PDF to under 1 MB?

Sometimes, depending on its original size and images. The tool shows the actual size after processing and does not offer an automatic 1 MB target. If it is still too large, check whether the recipient accepts another delivery method.

How can I compress a PDF for an email attachment?

First check your email service’s attachment limit. Compress the document, inspect the result and download it before attaching it to your message. This tool does not send emails or share files for you.

Why is a scanned PDF often so large?

Each page may be a large image, unlike a PDF with native text. Image recompression modes may help if the scan’s images are supported. Check small characters, stamps and numbers carefully.

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

It may already be optimized or contain unsupported images. If the output is larger, the tool offers the original. Repeatedly compressing the same result does not ensure further savings and can degrade images.

How can I compress a PDF on iPhone or Android?

Open this page in a recent browser, choose the document from your phone’s files and run compression. Processing uses device memory: for a large document, close other tabs or try a computer.

Do I need to install software to compress a PDF?

No. Use a recent browser with JavaScript enabled; no extension is required. This service is not offered as an offline app: loading the page and signing in require network access.

Can I use this tool for a confidential PDF?

PDF processing stays local: the compressor does not transmit the contents, filename or previews. Account sign-in traffic is separate. For sensitive work documents, also follow your organization’s rules and read our privacy policy.

Will the text still be selectable after compression?

Native text and vector elements retain their original form. Pages are not flattened into photographs. A scan containing only images will not become selectable, however: this tool does not perform OCR.

How should I check quality after compressing a PDF?

Open the result and original, zoom into small text, photos and charts, then check useful forms and links. For a document intended for printing, also inspect it at the planned size. See our PDF quality guide.

Can I compress a signed or password-protected PDF?

Encrypted files and detected digital signatures are rejected, as are XFA forms. This tool does not unlock documents. Changing a signed file can invalidate its signature; keep the original for legal and archival use.

Why does processing fail with a large file?

The input limit is 100 MiB, or 104,857,600 bytes, but available memory may impose a lower limit. Try a recent computer for a large document. A damaged file or unsupported PDF feature can also prevent processing.

Can I compress several PDFs at once?

No. This tool currently processes one document at a time. Download and check the first result before choosing the next file. Batch processing and shared folders are not offered.
