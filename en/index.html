Which PDF compression mode should you choose?

Start with High quality for everyday documents. Choose Lossless to avoid deliberate image changes, or Smaller file when reducing size matters more than image detail.

Is your PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Compression happens locally in your browser, using your device’s processing power. The PDF is not uploaded, stored on our servers, or sent to an AI service.

Does compression change PDF quality?

Image quality can change in High quality and Smaller file modes. Lossless avoids deliberate image changes. Native text and vectors stay in their original format; pages are not flattened into pictures.

Frequently asked questions

Is this PDF compressor free?

Yes. You can compress and compare without an account. Sign in for free to download your PDF.

Does compressing a PDF reduce its quality?

Lossless mode optimizes PDF structure without deliberately changing image pixels. High quality and Smaller file modes can change image detail. Native text and vector graphics are preserved. Always review your downloaded document before sharing it.

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

No. The compressor reads and processes your file locally in the browser. Account registration and login use a separate server connection, but your PDF is not included.

Why didn’t my PDF get smaller?

Some PDFs are already efficiently compressed. Others contain images that this compressor does not change. If optimization produces a larger file, we offer your original instead.

Can I compress a PDF on my phone?

Yes, in a modern supported browser. Large files may exceed your phone’s memory. Try a desktop browser if processing fails or the tab closes.

What about signed or password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted files are not supported. Changing a digitally signed PDF can invalidate its signature, so the compressor rejects PDFs with detected digital signatures. Keep the original document for legal or archival use.
